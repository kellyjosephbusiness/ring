Personal injury firms buying motor vehicle accident cases have two options. **Live transfers** put a screened accident caller on your intake line while they are still on the phone, and your team signs the case. **Pre-signed cases** arrive with the intake done and the retainer already signed. The right choice depends mostly on one question: do you have an intake team that can take calls and sign cases?

## When do MVA live transfers make sense?

Live transfers are for firms that already have intake staff and want more cases this week. Your intake team takes the call and signs the case, so you keep control of the conversation that turns a caller into a client.

Live transfers fit if:

- Your intake team can answer quickly during the hours you set.
- You want your own people to run the signing conversation.
- You track your sign rate and want to lower your cost per signed case with a stronger intake process.

Firms sign 25 to 35% of our MVA live transfers, higher in California. And because you get a guaranteed cost per signed case, a slow week at your intake desk does not cost you the order: if you do not hit the guaranteed cost, we keep sending transfers free until you do.

## When do pre-signed MVA cases make sense?

Pre-signed cases are for firms that want to grow caseload without adding intake staff. We run the ads, do the full intake, and sign the claimant to your retainer. You pay for signed retainers only.

Pre-signed cases fit if:

- You do not have intake staff, or they are at capacity.
- You want a fixed price per signed case.
- You want protection on fallout: any case that falls out inside 28 days is replaced.

## How do you compare the cost of the two?

Compare them on **cost per signed case**, not cost per call. A pre-signed case has a fixed price per signed case. For live transfers, divide the price per transfer by your sign rate: if your team signs 1 in 4 transfers, each signed case costs you 4 transfers, plus the time your intake team spends on the calls.

The better your intake team signs, the more live transfers favor you. If you would rather not depend on intake at all, pre-signed cases take that variable away.
