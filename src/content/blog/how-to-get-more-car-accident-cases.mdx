Personal injury firms get more car accident cases from seven main channels: **referrals, search (SEO), paid search, TV and radio, reviews, lead generation companies, and pay-per-call live transfers or pre-signed cases**. The first five build demand over months and depend on your own intake to convert it. The last two buy cases on demand, so you can add volume this week. Most growing firms run several at once and judge every channel by one number: cost per signed case.

## 1. Referrals

Referrals from past clients, other attorneys and medical providers tend to bring in cases that already trust you. They are hard to scale on demand, so most firms pair them with a paid channel.

## 2. Search engine optimization (SEO)

Pages built around what accident victims actually search, such as a dedicated car accident page for each city you serve, bring in callers for free once they rank. SEO takes months to pay off and the competition for accident terms is intense.

## 3. Paid search

Paid search ads put your firm in front of people searching right now. Accident victims often want help immediately, which suits paid search, but clicks on accident terms are among the most expensive in advertising, and your team still has to screen and sign every inquiry.

## 4. TV, radio and billboards

Broadcast builds a name people remember when they need a lawyer. It needs a large budget and a long run before it pays off, and you cannot target callers by case criteria.

## 5. Reviews and reputation

A strong review profile lifts every other channel: people compare firms before they call. Ask happy clients for reviews as part of closing the case.

## 6. Lead generation companies

Lead generation companies sell you MVA leads, usually as web form leads that your team calls back. They are fast to start, but quality varies widely between vendors, and form leads have to be reached and qualified from scratch. Read [MVA leads: what they are and how to buy them](/blog/mva-leads) before you choose one.

## 7. Live transfers and pre-signed cases

Pay-per-call vendors run the ads, screen every caller against your case criteria, and either transfer the caller to your intake line while they are still on the phone or sign them to your retainer. You pay per transfer or per signed case.

## Which channel should your firm add first?

| If you need… | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Cases this month, with an intake team in place | Live transfers |
| Cases this month, without adding intake staff | Pre-signed cases |
| A lower cost per case over the next year | SEO and referrals |
| A known name in your market | TV, radio and reviews |

Whatever you choose, track cost per signed case for every channel, and fix intake speed first: the fastest answer usually signs the case.
