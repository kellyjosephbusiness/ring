## What makes a call qualify?

Every vertical has one written intake script. A call qualifies when the caller passes that script's qualifying questions: the same questions callers from our own ads answer. You know what qualifies before a call goes live.

For example, an **MVA call** is checked for:

- An accident inside the buyer's window, 30 or 90 days.
- A caller who was not at fault.
- Treatment already received, or a doctor already seen.
- An at-fault driver with insurance.
- A claim inside the statute of limitations.

Insurance calls are checked against the line's criteria, such as the age range and coverage amount for final expense, or the state and ZIP code, vehicles and drivers for auto.

## How do payouts work?

Payouts are set per vertical. You know the payout for each vertical before you send a single call, and you get paid for every call that qualifies. There is no guessing about what counts: the intake script decides.

## How does publisher vetting work?

Every publisher in our network is vetted before calls go live. Tell us the verticals, states and sources you run calls from, and we review your sources before the first call. Vetting protects the buyers, and it protects you: everyone in the network is held to the same intake script.

## How do you start selling calls to RingOnDemand?

1. **Book a publisher intro call.** One short call.

4. **Get vetted.** We review your sources before calls go live.
5. **Send calls, get paid.** Every caller answers the same qualifying questions as the callers from our own ads, and you get paid for every call that qualifies.
