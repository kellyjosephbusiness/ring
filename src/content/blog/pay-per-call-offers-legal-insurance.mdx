## 2. Is the qualification script written down?

A buyer should tell you exactly what makes a call qualify before it goes live. Every vertical in our network has one written intake script, and a call qualifies when the caller passes its questions: the same ones callers from our own ads answer.

## 3. When do you learn the payout?

Before your first call. Our payouts are set per vertical and agreed on the intro call, and you get paid for every call that qualifies.

## 4. What traffic is allowed?

Clear traffic rules protect you and the buyer. In our network, every publisher is vetted before calls go live, every publisher's ads and creatives are approved before they run, robocalls, incentivized traffic and misleading ads are banned, and we audit sources and can remove a publisher at any time.

## 5. What compliance does the buyer require?

Legal and insurance buyers are regulated, so their publishers are too. Expect a serious buyer to require:

- **Consent.** Every caller agrees to be connected before a transfer, with third-party consent certificates captured.
- **Recording.** Every intake call is recorded.
- **Do Not Call.** Numbers scrubbed against the National Do Not Call Registry before anyone calls or texts them.
- **Medicare rules.** Medicare calls that follow CMS rules for third-party marketing organizations, including the required disclaimer.

Read [TCPA-compliant live transfers](/blog/tcpa-compliant-live-transfers) and our [compliance page](/compliance).
