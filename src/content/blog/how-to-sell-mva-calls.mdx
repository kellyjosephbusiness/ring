To **sell MVA calls**, a pay-per-call publisher sends motor vehicle accident callers to a buyer that pays for every call meeting its criteria. The calls that get paid are callers whose accident is recent, who were not at fault, who have been treated, whose other driver was insured, and whose claim is still inside the statute of limitations. Know the buyer's script, its payout and its traffic rules before you send a single call.

## What makes an MVA call qualify?

Every vertical in our network has one written intake script. For MVA, callers are checked for:

- An accident inside the buyer's window: 30 or 90 days.
- A caller who was not at fault.
- Treatment already received, or a doctor already seen.
- An at-fault driver with insurance.
- A claim inside the statute of limitations.

A call qualifies when the caller passes those questions, the same questions callers from our own ads answer. You know what qualifies before a call goes live.

## Who buys MVA calls?

Personal injury law firms, which either take the caller as a live transfer to their intake team or have the case signed to their retainer. We buy MVA calls for firms across the US, and your calls go to the largest insurance and legal organizations in the US.

## How are MVA call payouts set?

With us, payouts are set per vertical and agreed on the intro call, before you send a single call, and you get paid for every call that qualifies. When you compare any buyer, ask:

- **What is the payout, in writing, before I send traffic?**
- **What exactly makes a call qualify**, and can I see the script?
- **How fast do you pay**, and how are disputed calls handled?

## What traffic can you send?

Buyers care about how the caller was reached as much as about the call itself. In our network:

- Every publisher is **vetted before calls go live**.
- Every publisher's **ads and creatives are approved before they run**.
- **Robocalls, incentivized traffic and misleading ads are not allowed.**
- We **audit sources** and can remove a publisher at any time.
